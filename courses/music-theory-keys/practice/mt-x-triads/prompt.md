# Build triads

Write each triad in **root position**: root first, then the third, then the fifth.

- `c_major`: C major.
- `a_minor`: A minor.
- `d_major`: D major (it needs a black key).
- `b_diminished`: B diminished.
- `quality_of_e_g_b`: the quality of the triad E G B. Use one of `"major"`, `"minor"` or `"diminished"` (lowercase; capitals and extra spaces are ignored).

Formulas, in half steps from each note to the next: major = 4 then 3, minor = 3 then 4, diminished = 3 then 3.

Any spelling of the right pitch counts, so F# and Gb are both accepted.

You don't need to know Python: just type your answers between the brackets or quotes.
