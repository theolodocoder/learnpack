# Key signatures and the circle

Answer four questions about key signatures and the circle of fifths.

1. `sharps_in_d_major`: how many sharps D major has (a number).
2. `flats_in_b_flat_major`: how many flats Bb major has (a number).
3. `key_with_three_sharps`: the major key with three sharps (a note in quotes).
4. `next_key_clockwise_after_g`: the next key clockwise after G on the circle of fifths (a note in quotes).

Numbers go without quotes. Notes go in quotes, using `#` for sharp and `b` for flat.

You don't need to know Python: just type your answers between the brackets or quotes.
