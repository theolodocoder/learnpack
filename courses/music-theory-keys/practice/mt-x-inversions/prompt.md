# Write inversions

Write each chord **from the lowest note up**. Order matters here: E G C and C E G are the same chord in different positions.

- `c_major_first_inversion`: C major, first inversion.
- `c_major_second_inversion`: C major, second inversion.
- `f_major_first_inversion`: F major, first inversion.
- `lowest_note_of_g_second_inversion`: the lowest note of G major in second inversion.

Reminder: first inversion has the third on the bottom, second inversion has the fifth on the bottom.

Any spelling of the right pitch counts.

You don't need to know Python: just type your answers between the brackets or quotes.
