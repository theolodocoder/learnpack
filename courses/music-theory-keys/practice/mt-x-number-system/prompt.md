# Numbers to chord names

Turn number-system progressions into chord names, then write out every chord in C major.

Write each chord as text in quotes, separated by commas. A major chord is just its letter (`"G"`), a minor chord adds `m` (`"Em"`), and a diminished chord adds `dim` or `°` (`"Bdim"` or `"B°"`).

1. `g_1564`: 1-5-6-4 in the key of G.
2. `d_145`: 1-4-5 in the key of D.
3. `c_251`: 2-5-1 in the key of C.
4. `diatonic_triads_in_c`: the seven chords of C major, 1 to 7, in order.

Sharps can be written with `#` and flats with `b`; C# and Db both count, and capital or small letters are fine.

You don't need to know Python: just type your answers between the brackets or quotes.
