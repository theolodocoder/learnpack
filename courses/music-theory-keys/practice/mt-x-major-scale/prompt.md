# Build major scales

Use the formula **W W H W W W H** (whole, whole, half, whole, whole, whole, half) to write three major scales.

- `g_major`: the G major scale.
- `f_major`: the F major scale.
- `d_major`: the D major scale.

Write each as **7 notes** starting on the root (do not repeat the root at the end). Use `#` for sharp and `b` for flat.

Letter names matter here. A major scale uses each letter A to G exactly once, so F major needs **Bb**, not A#. The checks test the gaps between your notes and that every letter appears once.

You don't need to know Python: just type your answers between the brackets or quotes.
