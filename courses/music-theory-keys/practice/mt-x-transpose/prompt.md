# Move a progression to a new key

The band plays `["C", "Am", "F", "G"]` (1-6-4-5 in C). Move it for two different singers, then count a key change.

1. `up_whole_step`: the same progression moved up a whole step (2 half steps).
2. `in_b_flat`: the same progression in the key of Bb.
3. `half_steps_from_g_to_bb`: how many half steps you go up from G to Bb (a number, no quotes).

Write chords as text in quotes. Minor chords keep their `m`. Spelling with sharps or flats both count (Bb or A#), but for `in_b_flat` the flat names are what a chart in Bb would use.

You don't need to know Python: just type your answers between the brackets or quotes.
