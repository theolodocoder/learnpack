# Add sevenths to 1-6-2-5

A plain 1-6-2-5 in C is `C Am Dm G`. Gospel players rarely leave it plain: each chord gets its seventh.

Use exactly these suffixes:

| Chord in a major key | Suffix | Example in C |
|---|---|---|
| 1 and 4 | `maj7` | `Cmaj7` |
| 2, 3 and 6 | `m7` | `Am7` |
| 5 | `7` | `G7` |

1. `with_sevenths`: 1-6-2-5 in C, with sevenths.
2. `in_g_with_sevenths`: 1-6-2-5 in G, with sevenths.

Roots can be spelled either way (F# or Gb both count), but the suffix must be exact: `maj7`, `m7` or `7`.

You don't need to know Python: just type your answers between the brackets or quotes.
