# Relative minors

Every major key has a relative minor with the same notes. It starts on the **6th note** of the major scale, which is **3 half steps below** the major root. Going the other way, the relative major is 3 half steps **above** the minor root.

1. `relative_minor_of_c`: the relative minor of C major (just the root note, e.g. "B").
2. `relative_minor_of_g`: the relative minor of G major.
3. `relative_major_of_d_minor`: the relative major of D minor.
4. `a_natural_minor`: the A natural minor scale, 7 notes from A (formula W H W W H W W).

Write notes in quotes. Use `#` for sharp and `b` for flat.

You don't need to know Python: just type your answers between the brackets or quotes.
