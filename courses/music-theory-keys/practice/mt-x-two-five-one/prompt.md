# 2-5-1 in F and Bb

Write the 2-5-1 with sevenths in two keys that church and gospel songs use all the time.

The pattern is the same in every major key: `2m7  5 7  1maj7`. In C that is `Dm7 G7 Cmaj7`.

1. `f_251`: 2-5-1 in F.
2. `bb_251`: 2-5-1 in Bb.

Roots can be spelled either way (Bb or A# both count), but the suffix must be exact: `m7`, `7`, `maj7`.

You don't need to know Python: just type your answers between the brackets or quotes.
