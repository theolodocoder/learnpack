# Map a song in A

A worship leader hands you this chart, written in numbers, and says "we're in A".

```
Verse:   1 - 5 - 6 - 4
Chorus:  4 - 1 - 5 - 6
Bridge:  2 - 5 - 1
```

Write the chord names for each part in the key of A: `verse`, `chorus` and `bridge`.

Major chords are just the letter (`"A"`), minor chords add `m` (`"Bm"`). Sharps use `#`; F# and Gb both count, and capital or small letters are fine.

You don't need to know Python: just type your answers between the brackets or quotes.
