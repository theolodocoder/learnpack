# Map a bar of 12/8

A bar of 12/8 has 12 eighth-note pulses, felt as four groups of three: **ONE**-two-three **FOUR**-five-six **SEV**-en-eight **TEN**-eleven-twelve.

1. `groups`: the pulses 1 to 12 written as four lists of three, in order. The format is a list of lists, like `[[1, 2, 3], ...]`.
2. `accents`: the pulses you lean on, which are the first pulse of each group, as a list of numbers.

You don't need to know Python: just type your answers between the brackets or quotes.
