# Your scale sheet

## Goal

Build a one-page scale sheet for five of the most common keys in church, pop and Afro music: C, G, D, F and Bb major, plus their relative minors. Then play everything on it. This sheet becomes your reference for the chords and progressions sections.

## Requirements

- A keyboard: a real piano or keyboard, or any free piano app or website keyboard.
- Paper, a notebook page or a document you can print.
- Optional: your phone to record yourself.

## Step-by-step plan

1. **Write the major scales.** For C, G, D, F and Bb, write the seven letters from the root, then apply W W H W W W H and add sharps or flats. Check that each letter appears once (F major has Bb, not A#).
2. **Mark the steps.** Under each scale, write W or H between every pair of notes, including the step from the 7th back to the root.
3. **Count the accidentals.** Next to each key write how many sharps or flats it has and which ones (G: F#. D: F#, C#. F: Bb. Bb: Bb, Eb).
4. **Find the relative minors.** For each major key, take the 6th note: A, E, B, D and G. Write each relative minor's natural minor scale using W H W W H W W, and check it uses the same notes as its major key.
5. **Play the major scales.** Play each major scale up and down, right hand then left hand, slowly and evenly. Say the note names as you play.
6. **Play the relative minors.** Play each relative minor scale up and down. Notice it uses the same keys as its major, but feels different because the home note changes.
7. **Pair them.** For each key, play the major scale then its relative minor straight after. Record at least one pair.
8. **Place them on the circle.** Draw a circle of fifths and mark where your five keys sit: C at the top, G and D clockwise, F and Bb anticlockwise.

## Stretch goals

- Add A, E, Eb and Ab major and their relative minors to the sheet.
- Add the harmonic minor version (raised 7th) of each minor scale.
- Play each scale hands together at a steady tempo with a metronome.
- Take a song you know, work out its home note, and decide which key on your sheet it is in.
