# Learn a song by theory

## Goal

Work out a full song from a recording using only theory and your ear, write a complete number chart for it, and play it back on the keyboard.

## Requirements

- A song you have not played before, chosen by you. Work it out yourself; don't look up a chart.
- A one-page number chart with: the key, the time signature and tempo, every section in numbers, and the order of sections.
- The chord names in the original key written under the numbers.
- A note of any key change, borrowed chord or seventh you hear.
- A recording of you playing the song from your chart, without the original playing.

## Step-by-step plan

1. Listen to the whole song twice without playing. Note the sections.
2. Find the key note: hum the last note and the end of the chorus, then find it on the keyboard.
3. Follow the bass line in each section and write the bass notes.
4. Turn the bass notes into numbers using the scale of the key.
5. Play the default qualities (1, 4, 5 major; 2, 3, 6 minor) along with the song and fix any that clash.
6. Find the groove: count the beats, decide on two or three per beat, and measure the tempo.
7. Write the chart, with section names and bar lines and no lyrics.
8. Play along with the recording and fix mistakes, then record yourself playing the chart alone.

## Stretch goals

- Play the song from your chart in a second key.
- Add sevenths or a 2-5-1 where the song suggests one.
- Make a short groove in a DAW using your chart's chords and the song's time signature.
