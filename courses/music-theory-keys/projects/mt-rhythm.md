# Count and clap three songs

## Goal

Show that you can hear, count and play the groove of real songs: one in simple time, one in 3/4 or 6/8, and one with a 12/8 or triplet feel.

## Requirements

- Three songs you choose yourself:
  - one in 4/4 with a straight feel;
  - one in 3/4 or 6/8;
  - one in 12/8, or with a clear triplet or shuffle feel.
- For each song: the time signature, the tempo in beats per minute, and how each beat divides (in two or in three).
- A recording of yourself counting out loud and clapping along to each song for at least eight bars.
- A recording of yourself playing a simple keyboard pattern that fits each groove, for example rolled triads in threes for the 12/8 song.

## Step-by-step plan

1. Listen to each song and tap your foot. Count the beats in a bar.
2. Decide whether each beat splits into two or three. Name the time signature.
3. Use a tap-tempo metronome to find the tempo, and write it down.
4. Count out loud along with the song ("1 & 2 &" or "1 la li 2 la li") and clap on the main beats.
5. Choose a keyboard pattern for each song: straight eighths, a waltz pattern, or rolled triplets.
6. Practise each pattern with a metronome, then along with the song.
7. Record the counting-and-clapping and the keyboard pattern for each song.

## Stretch goals

- Program the drum groove of the 12/8 song in a DAW on a triplet grid.
- Clap the shuffle (first and third of each triplet) along with a blues song.
- Find a song where you can hear threes against twos, and clap both layers.
