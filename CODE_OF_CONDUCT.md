# Code of conduct

LearnPack is built in the open by people learning and teaching together. Be the kind of collaborator you'd want on your first pull request.

**Expected**
- Be respectful and patient, especially with beginners. Every question is a fair question.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person, and explain the why.
- Assume good intent, and accept correction gracefully.

**Not accepted**
- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere you represent the project. It follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

**Reporting:** contact the maintainer privately through [GitHub](https://github.com/theolodocoder). Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, or block anyone who breaks this code.
